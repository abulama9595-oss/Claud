# The real-estate tool v2 (`realestate-v2.html`, `re2-engine.js`, `re2-ui.js`, `tests/re2/`) — reference

Moved out of `CLAUDE.md` unchanged (the phase-by-phase account of the build: data model, engine per kind, properties, lenses and advisor, the page, the search). The header comment of `re2-engine.js` is the source of truth for the model; this file is the long-form reference. Read the section you need, not the whole file.

**The real-estate tool (v2)** — five project kinds, every property you own, three strategy lenses, a settle-or-keep advisor, a decision-first answer page; the landing page's real-estate card. Forked from the old files, which stay **frozen** as the "previous version" (deleted later; their scenarios import into this one). Own `?v=` tag (sha1 of engine.js + re2-engine.js + re2-ui.js) and storage keys `re2_scenarios` / `re2_current` / `re2_current_name`. **Data model (P1):** `inp.kind` ∈ `landVilla` · `readyVilla` · `land` · `landBuilding` · `readyBuilding` (`mode`/`acquire` are derived in `mergeRE` via `RE_KINDS`, no rental mode), `occupancy` liveLet/let + unit keys, land keys (`landSellYear`, `landSellCostPct`, `landZakat`, `whiteLandPct`, `whiteLandOtherArea`), `rentFreezeUntil` (default 2030-09), `readyLandShare`/`readyAge`, `propertyValueBasis` market/today, `samaBands` (SAMA's bands for a single borrower too), bank terms `landOnlyLtv`/`landTenure`/`landRateAdd`, `invLtv`/`invTenure`/`invRateAdd`, `dscrMin`; **`inp.properties[]`** `{id, name, value, ltv, canMortgage, rentNow, isHome, plan keep|sell, sellAt, letAfterMove, letYield, sellCostPct, existingLoanId}` replaces `ownedValue`/`ownHome*` (`reNormProps`; a loan `securedOn` a property is a mortgage); plans carry `mortgages: {[propertyId]: {when, month}}` instead of `eqWhen/eqMonth` (`rePlanMorts`). Synthetic defaults (`properties: []`, cash 2,000,000, `plRate` 6.5, `lmRate` 5.0). **`re2Import(oldInp)`** converts an old-tool scenario (also applied automatically by `mergeRE` when `re2IsOld`) so the new engine gives the old engine's numbers: old defaults filled in, `propertyValueBasis: "today"`, `samaBands: false`, `rentFreezeUntil: null`, `eqWhen` → `mortgages.p1`, rental → a let-entirely building (`legacyRental`). Golden: `computeRE({ asOf: "2026-10" })` → `landVilla`, `prj.total` 3087912, RENTING WINS, best −2836605 (the rent freeze is on by default and counts from `asOf`; with `rentFreezeUntil: null` the best is −2422044); `computeRE(re2Import({}))` → the old golden. Both parity keys ignore milestone events. **Your answer** now shows a verdict line (the recommended plan, the measures it wins, and its gap on the others), one "Compare by" row with the three pickers, then **three fixed cards** in a fixed order: least liability, richest, most cash and borrowing power. The cards never merge. A card whose plan an earlier card shows says "same plan as …". Each card has a "Show this plan" button. Print keeps the cards and leaves the table out. **Save and buy:** `reSaveAndBuy(inp, {maxWait, landWithBuild})` runs `RE_PLAN_PRESETS.allCash` at every month from the planned start up to `maxWait` (default 12) months later. While you wait, you keep renting and your savings build up. It returns `found`, the earliest start that works every month with no new loan (with `wait`, `delay`, `onHand`, `econAdv`, `rulesMet`), or `need`, the cash still missing at the last start. It is pure and does not touch `computeRE`. Tests: `savebuy.test.js`.

**Early payoff as soon as the cash allows:** a 20-year mortgage held to its term while spare cash earns less than the mortgage costs made short personal loans look better than they are. In one case the tool's pick bought out three old loans, one of them cheaper than the cash return, with the bank's maximum personal loans, while a plain purchase mortgage paid off early was clearly richer. `plan.prepayAfter: "asap"` now checks every month from move-in. It pays off each mortgage whose APR is above the net cash return once the cash above the cushion and three months' outgoings covers the payoff and its fee. A cheaper mortgage is kept. A numeric `prepayAfter` works as before. `optimizeRE` reruns its leading mixes that hold a mortgage and work (at most 8 per start) with "asap". It keeps the reruns that paid something off and ranks them with the rest, naming them "· pay the mortgage off early when your cash allows". `cleanPlan` carries the option. `opts.prepay: false` skips the reruns, and the optimizer parity test uses it to compare with the old tool's search; the default search must rank no lower. Summaries add `prepaid` and `prepaidAt`. The plan editor's early-payoff choice adds "As soon as your cash allows". Tests: `optimizer.test.js` (early payoff).

**Review batch 3:** **Top-ups:** `refiStyle` (bank terms; `RE_BASE` "topup", `re2Import` "consolidate"). Under "topup", a personal-loan step may refinance each old personal loan one-for-one with its own bank. The new instalment is the old one plus a share of the free room, dearest loan first, within the per-loan cap and the bank's ceiling. The new loan clears the old one and its fee, and the rest goes to your cash. Every subset of eligible loans is tried, with the same chooser. The loan count is unchanged, so no slot is used. The parts share the step's rate and tenure, so they add up to one facility, with `f.topups[] {id, name, oldBal, oldInst, payoff, amount, inst, net}`. Old loans carry `toppedUpAt`. Combining old loans into a new loan is allowed only when the new APR is below their balance-weighted APR. Under "consolidate" the step behaves as before. Mix names say "top-up of A + B, each with its own bank". **Lens ties** go to the recommended plan, then the earlier plan. **Save and buy:** `opts.scanTo` finds the first start beyond the wait (`later`), and `priceRise` is the all-in cost's growth a month. The box compares your monthly savings with that rise, names when your old loans end, and gives the first month without a wait limit. **The step-by-step** of a plan that runs out puts the shortfall before the purchase in that month, and stops there with "The plan stops here". **The answer page order:** verdict, plan picker, scoreboard table with column notes, Compare-by settings, then the three cards. A plan that runs out shows "runs out — needs X more" in the table. The side panel's plans table has notes too. **Wording:** `W.doneShort` / `doneLabel` say "project end" / "Project end" (land: "purchase" / "Land paid for"), and the helpers' and engine's texts say "at project end", "after the project end" and so on.

**Loan details:** each loan step and early payoff in "What happens, in order" carries `fac`, the facility key, and the step shows a "Details ›" link. Rows of new loans in "What you take on" have the link too. It opens `LoanDetails`, a side-sheet dialog (full screen on a phone; Esc or × closes it; not printed) with six sections: the loan (amount, borrower, security, rate as quoted, APR and effective yearly cost, tenure, instalment, dates, fees); why this size (the bank's maximum then and what limited it, and instalments as a % of income before and after); where the money goes (old loans cleared with their fees, or each top-up, and the net amount); what it costs; how it ends (including the early-payoff cash test); and year by year. `re2-ui.js`: `reFacSchedule(f, r, plan)` rebuilds the loan's month-by-month balance from its rate, instalment, start, the plan's extra payments, the early payoff (`prepaidAt`) or settlement (`settledAt`). `reFacDetail(r, key, p, prj)` gathers the sections. Test: `loandetail.test.js` checks that the schedules add up to `months.debtNew` every month and that the profit equals `interestPaid`.
