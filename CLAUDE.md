# Dental Clinic Feasibility Study — project guide

Interactive financial models for a dental venture in Riyadh, KSA. Pure single-file
React apps (no build step) sharing one engine. Hosted on GitHub Pages.

## Files
| File | What it is |
|------|-----------|
| `index.html` | Landing page — links the models (one card for the real-estate tool) |
| `redesign.html` | **Primary** clinic model — investor-study layout, live KPIs, print/PDF, "Ask the study" assistant. This is where clinic features are built. |
| `original.html` | Original clinic layout, **frozen** for comparison. Runs the same engine; do not add features here (only touch if a shared-engine change would break it). |

| `realestate.html` | Real-estate scenario tool (own engine `re-engine.js`, not the clinic engine; page helpers in `re-ui.js`). Two modes. **`home`**: one to eight time-phased financing *plans* (`inp.plans`, merged over `RE_PLAN_DEFAULTS`; the page adds them from a `RE_PLAN_PRESETS` template, a copy of a plan or a copy of the tool's pick, and deletes them with undo; at least one), plus **the tool's pick**: the optimizer's best mix for the planned start, run as one more plan by `computeRE(inp, { extraPlans: [plan] })` (appended to `results`, marked `r.tool`; it is ranked and can be the verdict's plan; without the option nothing changes). Each strategy is a sequence of four facilities: `lm` mortgage on the new land at purchase (buy-ready mode: the purchase mortgage, sized on `prj.price`) (`landLtv` 90% — purchase finance; the cash-raising mortgages use 70%; **no construction mortgage**), `eq` mortgage on a property the user already owns (`ownedValue × ownedLtv`, `eqWhen` start/build/month), `pl` personal loan (`plWhen`, `plCap`), `co` cash-out at completion (`cashoutLtv`, net of the land-mortgage balance still secured on the same home). **Bank terms are ONE set per loan type** on the inputs, not per strategy: `lmRate/lmTenure/lmFeesPct`, `eqRate/eqTenure/eqFeesPct`, `plRate/plTenure`, `coRate/coTenure/coFeesPct` (in `RE_BASE`); `mergeRE` migrates an older scenario once from the first enabled strategy that uses each loan (`reTermsFromPlans`, marks `termsV: 1`, keeps the old per-strategy keys); the page shows a one-time notice (`reTermsNotice`) and a "Bank terms" card at the top of Financing (flat → APR → effective yearly rate, `reEffYear`). **Personal loans include refinancing** (`takePersonal`): at `plWhen` the bank lends its MAXIMUM as one or two new personal loans (each instalment ≤ `perLoanCap` and each loan ≤ `plCap` — a capped first loan leaves its unused instalment to the second — within the bucket, never more than two personal loans running at once); every subset of eligible old personal loans (existing loans with `canRefi`, at least `refiMinMonths` (12) old via `ageMonths` + t, blank = old enough) is tried — including none — and the strategy's `refiMode` picks: `auto` (default) = best value (net cash − PV of the new loans' payments to the horizon and balance then + PV of the old loans' remaining payments and balance, discounted at `altReturn`), and `reRunPlan` reruns it with `cash` when that plan fails one of the user's rules (not executable, misses recovery, or cash at move-in below `minCashMoveIn`) while the most-cash choice meets more of them (`refiFallback`, `refiFallbackWhy`, an event kind `rf` in the month the personal loans are taken, just before that step — never pinned to month 0); `value`; `cash` (most cash in hand); `pick` (`refiPick {pl: [ids], pl2: [ids]}`, existing-loan ids as strings, `"pl"` = the strategy's earlier personal loan; ineligible picks are dropped with a note; replacing the loan list clears picks). Bought-out loans are cleared by the bank (no cash from the user), reported as `pl.refi {names, payoff, net}`, `pl.split`, `refiTotal`; the step's event names the NEW loans (one, or two with why each is its size — the per-loan limit / the bank's ceiling, then what is left of the room) apart from the old loans bought out ("It buys out 1 old loan, …"). A second personal-loan step `pl2` (`pl2Use`, at completion, after the cash-out) works the same way and can refinance the strategy's earlier PL once it is 12 months old; `months.plPay`/`debtPl` combine both. The separate `rf` facility is retired (legacy `rfWhen` maps to `plWhen`). Mortgages are never refinanced; **paying the new mortgages off early is an option** (`prepayAfter` = months after move-in, null = never): after that month's instalments, whole mortgages, dearest first, while cash − cost ≥ `cashBuffer` (results `prepaid`, `prepaidTotal`, `prepayFees`, counted in `newDebtInterest`); `extraMo` is a voluntary extra payment on the land mortgage, kept out of the DBR test and peak instalments (`extraPay`). **Every new loan is taken at the bank's maximum** (user rule; `mergeRE` forces `lmMode/eqMode/plMode/coMode = "max"` and the simulation calls `originate(..., "max")`; legacy need/fixed are read as max); **amounts are computed, not sliders**: capacity = min(LTV cap, product cap, principal the DBR room allows at that rate/tenure) where room = what is left of the ONE bucket `dbrCap × income − all instalments`, further capped per facility: **all mortgages together** ≤ `mortgageCap` (55%) (room = cap − the other mortgages already running), one personal loan ≤ `perLoanCap` (33%), two personal loans allowed when `plSplit` (the engine reports `pl.split`). Income and living costs may grow (`incomeGrowth`, `livingGrowth` %/yr, yearly steps, default 0, same on both sides); the bank limits and the DBR % follow that month's income. **تضامن — a joint mortgage with your spouse (`inp.joint = {on, income, incomeGrowth (null = yours), redf, loans}`, off by default; off = bit-identical):** one household — the spouse's salary joins `inc` (= `incA` + `incB`) and their loans (`joint.loans`, owner "B", ids `s…`, normalised by `reNormLoans` like yours) run through household cash on both sides; the bank tests each person on their own income with `reSamaCap(income, dbrCap, redf)` (SAMA Responsible Lending Principles ¶15–17: 55% up to 15,000 — 65% MoH/REDF-supported —, 65% from 15,000 to 25,000, from 25,000 the bank's own policy; never above `dbrCap`) and `mortgageCap` per person; the land/purchase mortgage and the cash-out are joint (`capacityJoint`: room = yours + your spouse's, each min(limit − own debts, mortgage limit − own mortgages); instalment split `shareA` = your room / both, fixed when taken; nothing while your own debts are over your limit); a mortgage on a property you own stays yours; **personal loans are in one person's name** — the plan's `plBy` (`me` default / `spouse` / `both`; ignored without تضامن; the plan editor's "Personal loans taken by: Me · My spouse · Both of us" with today's maximum for each from `capTimeline` `maxTwoPl`/`maxTwoPlB`): yours `pl`/`pl2` (labelled "Your personal loan…" with تضامن), your spouse's `spl`/`spl2` (owner "B", `takePersonal` on that person's income, room (`dbrTargetB`, `perLoanTargetB`, their SAMA band), their own two-loan rule and their own old loans to buy out — `refiPick.spl/spl2`; event kind `spl` "Your spouse's personal loan — on your spouse's income of X/mo (one loan's instalment ≤ 33% = …; all their debts ≤ …)"; `shareOf` puts them on your spouse, so `payB`/`dbrB`; `months.plPay`/`debtPl`/`newInt` include them; `r.spl`/`r.spl2` exist only when used, and with them off or `plBy` `me` every number is unchanged); "both" = each of you the bank's maximum in the same month; a gap closed by both moves both together (`covered.keys`, "yours X + your spouse's Y"); the two-part split is only for `me`; `loanWorthRE` leaves one person's loan out (internal `plan._off`); `optimizeRE` tries me / spouse / both on every mix with a personal-loan step when your spouse earns (48 → 120 mixes without an owned property, 192 → 480 with one; `cleanPlan.plBy` from what was drawn); your spouse's loans may be paid off from your cash only for a joint mortgage while you are within your limit; results `months[].incA/incB/payA/payB/dbrA/dbrB/capA/capB/bankCap`, `peakDbrA/B`, per-person `dbrOk`; `household.joint` (each one's existing debts, limits, room) and spouse warnings (over their limit; SAMA's 45% non-mortgage below 25,000 and 33.33% salary-deducted); `capTimeline` rows add `instB/incomeB/roomB/mortRoomB` (joint mortgage room = both) and your spouse's personal-loan room `pl1RoomB/pl2RoomB/maxOnePlB/maxTwoPlB`; stress/tornado scale both incomes. Page: a "تضامن" box in "Your money", "Your spouse's loans" table (`loanTable`, also an import target), both persons in the rails, the room table, the "Today" row, the limit chart (`bankCap`) and the peak row. **Optional features, all off by default (every saved scenario unchanged with them off):** `partRentAnnual` (annex/floor let while you live there, SAR/yr from the month after move-in, grows with `rentGrowth`; off when the whole home is let); `ownHomePlan` keep/let/sell for the home you own and live in today (only when `rentNow` = 0, `ownedValue` > 0 and the new home is not let; it appreciates; let = `ownHomeYield` % of its value a year from move-in; sell = at move-in, value − `ownHomeSellCostPct` − repayment of a strategy's `eq` mortgage (fee in `newDebtInterest`), no `eq` mortgage after; its value is deducted on the strategy side in both measures via `months.ownGone`; result `ownSale`); `cashZakatPct` (the monthly growth rate becomes ((1+r)(1−z))^(1/12) − 1 on every cash ledger and in today's-money discounting; `r.netReturn`); per-loan `rateType: "flat"` for existing loans (converted to APR in `mergeRE` over the whole term = `ageMonths` + `monthsLeft`, then marked `apr` with `flatRate` kept). Liquidity results: `reserveMonths` (low point ÷ that month's outgoings), `thinMonths` (surplus < 5% of income), `tightest`. `optimizeRE(inp, {maxDelay, step, landWithBuild})` searches 192 mixes per start month (land mortgage yes/no × PL at build yes/no × PL at completion yes/no × existing-property none/build/now/land × cash-out yes/no × settle policy asNeeded/forMax/none, all at max) built on `RE_PLAN_DEFAULTS` + the shared terms (nothing borrowed from Strategy 1), ranked by standing → rules met (`ok` = executable + recovery + move-in line; summaries carry `onHand`, `moveInOk`) → `econAdv` → peak instalment; `opts.plannedOnly` / `opts.delays` for whole starts, or `{delays:[d], slice:[a,b], planned}` → `{part, combos}` then `{merge:{delay, planned, parts}}` → `{start}` (the page runs 48 mixes per tick: planned start first, every start only on the "When" step); returns per-start bests (`starts`, `planned`, `earliest`, each with `best.bucket` monthly instalment composition incl. `inc`, and `best.cleanPlan`); the UI shows it in Financing (planned start + bucket area chart) and When to start (per-start table), debounced 350 ms. `need` = remaining project spend − (cash − `cashBuffer`). Existing loans (`inp.existingLoans`, table + JSON/CSV import) have a `type` — `personal` (default; one of the two personal loans a person may run, refinanceable, may be paid off for room) or `mortgage` (never a slot, never bought out or settled for room; its instalment counts in the `mortgageCap` test with the new mortgages — `existMortFor`, `capTimeline` `mortRoom`, `household.mortgageRoom`, a warning when it already passes the limit; untyped = personal, so older scenarios are unchanged) — and can have a planned `settleAt` month (it happens on the not-building side too: `renterSettle`) and a `canSettle` flag; `household.warnings` flags an instalment that doesn't fit the balance, rate and months left. `settlePolicy` none/asNeeded/forMax (legacy `all` = asNeeded): personal loans are paid off from cash ONLY to make room for a MORTGAGE (lm/eq/co) — asNeeded greedily until it covers the funding gap, forMax (mortgage sized max) until it is no longer DBR-bound, i.e. reaches its LTV/product cap — paid from the user's OWN cash on hand before the mortgage is granted (a land-purchase mortgage goes straight to the seller); a loan the cash can't cover is skipped and the next one tried (flag + event) — never for a personal loan or a refinance; the gap is re-measured after each cash settlement. Early-settlement fee (`reSettleCost`) = the next min(`settleFeeMonths`, months left) months of profit on the declining balance. Rates quoted `rateQuote` apr|flat (hamish → APR via `reFlatToApr`). Project (`reProject`): timing `landMonth`, `buildStart`, `costInflation`; built area = land × `builtPct` × `floors`; value = land + building (`prj.valueAt(t)`, `prj.landValueAt(t)`): the land, priced at today's price grown at `appreciation` to `landMonth`, keeps growing at `appreciation`; the building is worth (hard + soft + finish) × (1 + `devMarginPct`) (`prj.buildValue` — the uplift is on what is built, not the land), accrues with progress during the build and from completion loses `buildDeprPct` (default 2) % of that a year, straight-line, never below 0; contingency, overrun, VAT, RETT and fees add no value; a ready home bought as such keeps price + finishing growing with the market (`readyParts`, internal, values the ready villa what-if as land + a building that already ages); `landBrokerPct` (2.5%) paid with the land, not in value, RETT or LTV (`prj.landBroker`); `overrunPct` (default 0) on hard + soft + contingency + finish, plus VAT, spread over the build, adds cost not value (`prj.overrun`). Simulated month by month as a household cash balance; a plan whose cash goes negative is "not executable" — unless one of its own loans closes the gap (user rule, `reCarryOut` inside `reRunPlan`): **covered by a loan** = the closing loan, if it can move (`pl`, `pl2`, `eq` via the internal `plan._at`), is taken in the month the cash first runs short (bank's maximum then, refinancing what it may then) and the plan works every month (`covered`, `notes[0]`, event kind `cover` — the row says how short the cash would be and what the loan brings: "by up to P … The bank lends X: the P deficit is covered by a loan", facility `takenEarly` = planned month); when that is not enough and the step is a personal-loan step that may be two loans (`plSplit`) while the plan's other personal-loan step is unused, it is **taken in two parts** (`reCarryOut` → internal `plan._split` / `_oneLoan`, texts by `reSplitNote`): one loan on `pl` in the month the cash runs short (the bank's maximum for one loan then) and the rest on `pl2` at the planned month — itself moved earlier when the cash runs short again — each at the bank's maximum when taken, in two different months; kept when the plan then works every month, or when it needs less bridge money than the step taken whole (`covered.split`, `covered.parts`, `covered.partial`; facilities `part` 1/2, labels "…, first part" / "…, second part"; the plan itself is unchanged); otherwise **bridge money** (rerun with `plan._bridge`: a short balance costs `bridgeRate` % APR, default null = the personal-loan APR, per month in `months.bridgeInt`, taken from `cash` and `ccash`, in `newDebtInterest`) when every gap (`reGaps(months, facilities, ownSale)`: cash < 0 until a month a loan or the home sale lands) is still closed — `bridged`, `bridges[{from, to, peak, peakT, cost, closedBy, closedByLabel, reason}]`, `bridgeCost`, `bridgePeak`, `bridgeRate`, flag "Needs bridge money: … No loan can cover it in time: {reason}", events kind `bridge`; a gap no loan closes still runs out (the straight run, unchanged). `feasible` stays strict (never short); `reStanding(r)` = 2 works every month (covered included) / 1 bridged / 0 runs out. **Two measures, both shown.** (1) **"Everything grows"** — how strategies are ranked and judged (R1b): per month `econNW` = household `cash` (salary in; living, rent, every instalment, project, payoffs, fees out; grows at `altReturn` from month 1 while positive) + home value − all debt; `econBase` = the not-building household's `rCash` (same salary, living costs, old loans, planned settlements, same growth) − the old-loan debt it still owes; `econAdv` = difference; results `econEnd`, `econBaseEnd`, `econAdv`, `econPV` (= econAdv / (1+alt)^H, today's money), `econBreakEven`; `reEconFlows(r)` / `reEconIRR(r)` give the return on building (null when undefined, computed only for strategies on screen). ## Engine model — how revenue & pay work (current logic)
Revenue is **dentist-driven, chair-capped**, not chair-driven:
- `revPerChair` = monthly production of a **full-time salaried dentist** (works all `clinicDays`); part-timers scale by `dentistDays/clinicDays`.
- `seniorRevPerChair` = each senior's **full monthly book**, earned **regardless of days worked** (they bring their own caseload). Days only set chair occupancy & cost allocation. Independent slider from `revPerChair`.
- **Capacity cap with SENIOR PRIORITY**: chairs offer `chairs × clinicDays` chair-days/week. Seniors are rostered first (they cost nothing idle and bring their own patients); salaried dentists fill the remainder and their production scales to the days they actually get. Shortfalls reported: `salariedDisplacedDays` and `seniorRealization < 1`.
- Derived output `revPerChairFull` = production capacity ÷ chairs (sanity-check vs ~160K KSA norm), NOT an input.

Two pay tiers:
- **Salaried**: base salary + `profitShare`% of the profit on **their OWN production** (their collected billings − their salary − their share of clinic costs: materials on their production + operating costs by occupied chair-days). NOT a cut of the whole-clinic pool. With **0 seniors this reduces exactly to 35% of whole-clinic operating profit → bit-identical to the pre-two-tier model.**
- **Senior**: no salary; paid `seniorProdPct`% on `seniorPayBasis` ∈ {`gross` = % of collected production, `netmat` = net of materials & lab, `profit` = net of materials + allocated operating costs}, floored by `seniorMinMo` guarantee. Covered by the clinic malpractice policy (premiums count both tiers). Optional case-mix comes purely from the higher `seniorRevPerChair`.
- The margin the clinic keeps on senior books accrues to the **owner (EBITDA)**, not the salaried pool.

Both `compute()` (annual P&L + returns) and the 24-month monthly cash engine implement these; keep them in sync. `compute()` merges saved scenarios over `BASE_INPUTS`, so new input keys are backward-compatible (legacy `seniorRevMult` is still honored).
