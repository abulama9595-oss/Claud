Two modes. **`home`**: one to eight time-phased financing *plans* (`inp.plans`, merged over `RE_PLAN_DEFAULTS`; the page adds them from a `RE_PLAN_PRESETS` template, a copy of a plan or a copy of the tool's pick, and deletes them with undo; at least one), plus **the tool's pick**: the optimizer's best mix for the planned start, run as one more plan by `computeRE(inp, { extraPlans: [plan] })` (appended to `results`, marked `r.tool`; it is ranked and can be the verdict's plan; without the option nothing changes). Each strategy is a sequence of four facilities: `lm` mortgage on the new land at purchase (buy-ready mode: the purchase mortgage, sized on `prj.price`) (`landLtv` 90% — purchase finance; the cash-raising mortgages use 70%; **no construction mortgage**), `eq` mortgage on a property the user already owns (`ownedValue × ownedLtv`, `eqWhen` start/build/month), `pl` personal loan (`plWhen`, `plCap`), `co` cash-out at completion (`cashoutLtv`, net of the land-mortgage balance still secured on the same home). **Bank terms are ONE set per loan type** on the inputs, not per strategy: `lmRate/lmTenure/lmFeesPct`, `eqRate/eqTenure/eqFeesPct`, `plRate/plTenure`, `coRate/coTenure/coFeesPct` (in `RE_BASE`); `mergeRE` migrates an older scenario once from the first enabled strategy that uses each loan (`reTermsFromPlans`, marks `termsV: 1`, keeps the old per-strategy keys); the page shows a one-time notice (`reTermsNotice`) and a "Bank terms" card at the top of Financing (flat → APR → effective yearly rate, `reEffYear`). **Personal loans include refinancing** (`takePersonal`): at `plWhen` the bank lends its MAXIMUM as one or two new personal loans (each instalment ≤ `perLoanCap` and each loan ≤ `plCap` — a capped first loan leaves its unused instalment to the second — within the bucket, never more than two personal loans running at once); every subset of eligible old personal loans (existing loans with `canRefi`, at least `refiMinMonths` (12) old via `ageMonths` + t, blank = old enough) is tried — including none — and the strategy's `refiMode` picks: `auto` (default) = best value (net cash − PV of the new loans' payments to the horizon and balance then + PV of the old loans' remaining payments and balance, discounted at `altReturn`), and `reRunPlan` reruns it with `cash` when that plan fails one of the user's rules (not executable, misses recovery, or cash at move-in below `minCashMoveIn`) while the most-cash choice meets more of them (`refiFallback`, `refiFallbackWhy`, event); `value`; `cash` (most cash in hand); `pick` (`refiPick {pl: [ids], pl2: [ids]}`, existing-loan ids as strings, `"pl"` = the strategy's earlier personal loan; ineligible picks are dropped with a note; replacing the loan list clears picks). Bought-out loans are cleared by the bank (no cash from the user), reported as `pl.refi {names, payoff, net}`, `pl.split`, `refiTotal`. A second personal-loan step `pl2` (`pl2Use`, at completion, after the cash-out) works the same way and can refinance the strategy's earlier PL once it is 12 months old; `months.plPay`/`debtPl` combine both. The separate `rf` facility is retired (legacy `rfWhen` maps to `plWhen`). Mortgages are never refinanced; **paying the new mortgages off early is an option** (`prepayAfter` = months after move-in, null = never): after that month's instalments, whole mortgages, dearest first, while cash − cost ≥ `cashBuffer` (results `prepaid`, `prepaidTotal`, `prepayFees`, counted in `newDebtInterest`); `extraMo` is a voluntary extra payment on the land mortgage, kept out of the DBR test and peak instalments (`extraPay`). **Every new loan is taken at the bank's maximum** (user rule; `mergeRE` forces `lmMode/eqMode/plMode/coMode = "max"` and the simulation calls `originate(..., "max")`; legacy need/fixed are read as max); **amounts are computed, not sliders**: capacity = min(LTV cap, product cap, principal the DBR room allows at that rate/tenure) where room = what is left of the ONE bucket `dbrCap × income − all instalments`, further capped per facility: **all mortgages together** ≤ `mortgageCap` (55%) (room = cap − the other mortgages already running), one personal loan ≤ `perLoanCap` (33%), two personal loans allowed when `plSplit` (the engine reports `pl.split`). Income and living costs may grow (`incomeGrowth`, `livingGrowth` %/yr, yearly steps, default 0, same on both sides); the bank limits and the DBR % follow that month's income. **Optional features, all off by default (every saved scenario unchanged with them off):** `partRentAnnual` (annex/floor let while you live there, SAR/yr from the month after move-in, grows with `rentGrowth`; off when the whole home is let); `ownHomePlan` keep/let/sell for the home you own and live in today (only when `rentNow` = 0, `ownedValue` > 0 and the new home is not let; it appreciates; let = `ownHomeYield` % of its value a year from move-in; sell = at move-in, value − `ownHomeSellCostPct` − repayment of a strategy's `eq` mortgage (fee in `newDebtInterest`), no `eq` mortgage after; its value is deducted on the strategy side in both measures via `months.ownGone`; result `ownSale`); `cashZakatPct` (the monthly growth rate becomes ((1+r)(1−z))^(1/12) − 1 on every cash ledger and in today's-money discounting; `r.netReturn`); per-loan `rateType: "flat"` for existing loans (converted to APR in `mergeRE` over the whole term = `ageMonths` + `monthsLeft`, then marked `apr` with `flatRate` kept). Liquidity results: `reserveMonths` (low point ÷ that month's outgoings), `thinMonths` (surplus < 5% of income), `tightest`. `optimizeRE(inp, {maxDelay, step, landWithBuild})` searches 192 mixes per start month (land mortgage yes/no × PL at build yes/no × PL at completion yes/no × existing-property none/build/now/land × cash-out yes/no × settle policy asNeeded/forMax/none, all at max) built on `RE_PLAN_DEFAULTS` + the shared terms (nothing borrowed from Strategy 1), ranked executable → rules met (`ok` = executable + recovery + move-in line; summaries carry `onHand`, `moveInOk`) → `econAdv` → peak instalment; `opts.plannedOnly` / `opts.delays` for whole starts, or `{delays:[d], slice:[a,b], planned}` → `{part, combos}` then `{merge:{delay, planned, parts}}` → `{start}` (the page runs 48 mixes per tick: planned start first, every start only on the "When" step); returns per-start bests (`starts`, `planned`, `earliest`, each with `best.bucket` monthly instalment composition incl. `inc`, and `best.cleanPlan`); the UI shows it in Financing (planned start + bucket area chart) and When to start (per-start table), debounced 350 ms. `need` = remaining project spend − (cash − `cashBuffer`). Existing loans (`inp.existingLoans`, table + JSON/CSV import) can have a planned `settleAt` month (it happens on the not-building side too: `renterSettle`) and a `canSettle` flag; `household.warnings` flags an instalment that doesn't fit the balance, rate and months left. `settlePolicy` none/asNeeded/forMax (legacy `all` = asNeeded): personal loans are paid off from cash ONLY to make room for a MORTGAGE (lm/eq/co) — asNeeded greedily until it covers the funding gap, forMax (mortgage sized max) until it is no longer DBR-bound, i.e. reaches its LTV/product cap — paid from the user's OWN cash on hand before the mortgage is granted (a land-purchase mortgage goes straight to the seller); a loan the cash can't cover is skipped and the next one tried (flag + event) — never for a personal loan or a refinance; the gap is re-measured after each cash settlement. Early-settlement fee (`reSettleCost`) = the next min(`settleFeeMonths`, months left) months of profit on the declining balance. Rates quoted `rateQuote` apr|flat (hamish → APR via `reFlatToApr`). Project (`reProject`): timing `landMonth`, `buildStart`, `costInflation`; built area = land × `builtPct` × `floors`; value = land + building (`prj.valueAt(t)`, `prj.landValueAt(t)`): the land, priced at today's price grown at `appreciation` to `landMonth`, keeps growing at `appreciation`; the building is worth (hard + soft + finish) × (1 + `devMarginPct`) (`prj.buildValue` — the uplift is on what is built, not the land), accrues with progress during the build and from completion loses `buildDeprPct` (default 2) % of that a year, straight-line, never below 0; contingency, overrun, VAT, RETT and fees add no value; a ready home bought as such keeps price + finishing growing with the market (`readyParts`, internal, values the ready villa what-if as land + a building that already ages); `landBrokerPct` (2.5%) paid with the land, not in value, RETT or LTV (`prj.landBroker`); `overrunPct` (default 0) on hard + soft + contingency + finish, plus VAT, spread over the build, adds cost not value (`prj.overrun`). Simulated month by month as a household cash balance; a plan whose cash goes negative is "not executable". **Two measures, both shown.** (1) **"Everything grows"** — how strategies are ranked and judged (R1b): per month `econNW` = household `cash` (salary in; living, rent, every instalment, project, payoffs, fees out; grows at `altReturn` from month 1 while positive) + home value − all debt; `econBase` = the not-building household's `rCash` (same salary, living costs, old loans, planned settlements, same growth) − the old-loan debt it still owes; `econAdv` = difference; results `econEnd`, `econBaseEnd`, `econAdv`, `econPV` (= econAdv / (1+alt)^H, today's money), `econBreakEven`; `reEconFlows(r)` / `reEconIRR(r)` give the return on building (null when undefined, computed only for strategies on screen). (2) **Rule-7 net worth (user rule)** = `ccash` (own cash + every loan − land, construction, early payoffs, fees; GROWS at `altReturn` from today (month 1) while positive — both sides grow from today (R7) — never touched by instalments) − new-loan instalments (plain cost, `newInstCum`) + old-loan instalments no longer paid (`oldSavedCum` = baseline schedule − actual) + rent from letting the home (`rentInCum`) + rent no longer paid (`rentSavedCum`) − upkeep + home value (NO selling costs) − new-loan debt still owed + `oldDebtGap` (old-loan balance the not-building side still owes minus this strategy's, M6). Baseline (`bcash`) = own cash grown at `altReturn` from today, minus planned settlements. The rule-6 "cash on hand at completion" stays growth-free: `ccash − cRetCum` (= `fundingBridgeRows(r, occ).total` in `re-ui.js`; `.ret` is the growth). Letting out: `rentOutPct` (% of `prj.total` per year) or `rentOutFixed` (SAR/yr, overrides), from the month after completion, grows with `rentGrowth`, added to household cash too; results expose `annualRentOut`, `rentInTotal`. The household `cash` (with salary) drives feasibility/min cash; growth is never a charge on a shortfall, and rent received arrives after that month's growth. **Ranking**: executable first; among executable plans, the more of the user's two rules a plan meets the higher — cash back at the buffer within `recoveryMonths` of move-in (`recoveryOk`), and **cash at move-in** (`onHandAtMoveIn`, no growth anywhere = `loansLeftAtMoveIn` — rule 6: own cash + every loan − costs = `ccash − cRetCum` in the move-in month — + `salaryToMoveIn` — salary − living − rent + rent received − every instalment − upkeep, month 1 → move-in; = that month's bank balance − the return earned on it) at least `minCashMoveIn` (default 0, a setting in "Your money"; `moveInOk`, flagged when missed) — then `econAdv`; with every plan above the line the order is the earlier one. `bestFeasible` = the first executable plan. **Verdict** is judged on the recommended (executable) strategy `bestFeasible` by `econAdv`: `BUILDING WINS` / `NOT BUILDING WINS` (`BUYING…` in buy mode) when no rent is paid today, `OWNING WINS` / `RENTING WINS` only when the baseline really rents (`rentNow > 0`), `NO EXECUTABLE STRATEGY` if none works; a move-in after the horizon is flagged. `timingSweepRE` reruns one strategy (`reRunOne`) for every start (3-month steps plus the planned start) and picks the earliest that meets the most of the user's rules (all of them if any start does; rows carry `onHand`, `moveInOk`); `tornadoRE` flexes drivers ±20% on `econAdv`. `household.capTimeline` shows when borrowing room opens up (priced at the shared terms; a row when a loan ends or income steps up; personal-loan columns respect the two-loan rule — existing loans count — and `plCap`). **`rental`**: capital stacks (`inp.options`, 1–8 on the page, merged over `RE_OPTION_PRESETS`) → levered/unlevered IRR, NPV, DSCR, yields. ## Engine model — how revenue & pay work (current logic)
Revenue is **dentist-driven, chair-capped**, not chair-driven:
- `revPerChair` = monthly production of a **full-time salaried dentist** (works all `clinicDays`); part-timers scale by `dentistDays/clinicDays`.
- `seniorRevPerChair` = each senior's **full monthly book**, earned **regardless of days worked** (they bring their own caseload). Days only set chair occupancy & cost allocation. Independent slider from `revPerChair`.
- **Capacity cap with SENIOR PRIORITY**: chairs offer `chairs × clinicDays` chair-days/week. Seniors are rostered first (they cost nothing idle and bring their own patients); salaried dentists fill the remainder and their production scales to the days they actually get. Shortfalls reported: `salariedDisplacedDays` and `seniorRealization < 1`.
- Derived output `revPerChairFull` = production capacity ÷ chairs (sanity-check vs ~160K KSA norm), NOT an input.

Two pay tiers:
- **Salaried**: base salary + `profitShare`% of the profit on **their OWN production** (their collected billings − their salary − their share of clinic costs: materials on their production + operating costs by occupied chair-days). NOT a cut of the whole-clinic pool. With **0 seniors this reduces exactly to 35% of whole-clinic operating profit → bit-identical to the pre-two-tier model.**
- **Senior**: no salary; paid `seniorProdPct`% on `seniorPayBasis` ∈ {`gross` = % of collected production, `netmat` = net of materials & lab, `profit` = net of materials + allocated operating costs}, floored by `seniorMinMo` guarantee. Covered by the clinic malpractice policy (premiums count both tiers). Optional case-mix comes purely from the higher `seniorRevPerChair`.
- The margin the clinic keeps on senior books accrues to the **owner (EBITDA)**, not the salaried pool.

Both `compute()` (annual P&L + returns) and the 24-month monthly cash engine implement these; keep them in sync. `compute()` merges saved scenarios over `BASE_INPUTS`, so new input keys are backward-compatible (legacy `seniorRevMult` is still honored).

## Golden rule: regression
Any engine change **must leave the default scenario (and 0-senior scenarios) bit-identical**. Baseline check:
```
node -e "eval(require('fs').readFileSync('engine.js','utf8')); const m=compute({});
console.log(Math.round(m.totalRaise), m.irr, Math.round(m.npv), m.years.map(y=>Math.round(y.ebitda)))"
```
Default must give: totalRaise **9033**, IRR **~0.6717**, NPV **32980**, verdict **STRONG**, Y5 EBITDA **6875**.
(NPV was 32981 before the Y5 IT-depreciation fix — IT has a 4-year life, so Y5 dep drops 455→405, raising Y5 zakat ~1.25 and nudging NPV down ~0.7.)
